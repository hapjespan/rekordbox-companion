# Booking Structures are hand-designed by the DJ, the app only suggests tracks

The kickoff assumed a generator that proposes a folder/playlist taxonomy from
profile tags. The owner rejected that in phase 1: the structure itself (genre and
theme playlists, Set Phases vooravond/mid/prime/sluit, a Run-of-Show folder with
Moment Playlists) is the DJ's own craft and is designed freely in the companion.
The app's contribution is per-playlist track Suggestions ranked by Play Count and
filtered by Enriched Genre and BPM, curated by the DJ before anything is Applied.
Reusable structure templates are explicitly deferred to a later version. Decided
in phase 1 grilling (D8), 2026-08-16.
